THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Before we talk.

Does Trade Gate buy products?+

No. Trade Gate acts as a commercial intermediary between manufacturers and retailers. We help prepare your offer, support buyer conversations and develop retail relationships. We do not buy products.

Can you help us find an importer in Poland?+

Yes. As part of our work, we help find importers suited to your product and target sales channels. Trade Gate acts as an intermediary and does not buy the goods.

Do you only work in the listed categories?+

No. We work across virtually all categories. The groups on this page are examples. Tell us about your product so we can assess fit and the potential scope of collaboration.

Do you work with manufacturers outside Poland?+

Yes. We support international manufacturers preparing to enter Polish retail and Polish brands developing their retail presence.

How are scope and fees agreed?+

Individually, after we understand your product, its readiness for retail and the support you require.

Can you guarantee a retail listing?+

No. The retailer makes the listing decision. Our role is to prepare a professional offer, support conversations and carry out the agreed work.

Can you help us enter all of Europe?+

Our specialism is the Polish market. If you are planning Central European expansion, tell us which countries you are targeting. Geographic scope must be agreed separately.

What should we include in an enquiry?+

A catalogue or website link, product category, current markets and target sales channels. We can clarify price positioning, availability and supply readiness in the next conversation. Confidential documentation can be discussed separately.
